## The problem

Frame the standalone risk spreadsheet problem: entries are free text,
written once, with nothing keeping them aligned to the architecture they
describe.

## The concept

What Bowtie actually is: the bow-tie method, causes and consequences either
side of a risk event, controls along each path, rolled up into a risk
matrix.

## How it works

Explain how a cause, exposure or control can reference a real zone, device
or flow from a bound Tetra model, and what that buys over free text.

## Why it matters

Set up the payoff that lands in the Metron paper: this is also where
promoted Findings arrive as new Causes, so the register updates from
compliance work rather than only from manual entry.
